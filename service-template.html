The Treatment

A facial tailored entirely to your skin

The CURRENT Signature Facial begins with a thorough skin analysis so every step is chosen for your unique concerns. We layer the most effective elements of advanced skincare — dermaplaning, gentle extractions, a custom chemical peel, and LED light therapy — into one seamless, results-driven experience.

It finishes the way every great facial should: with a calming face mask and a soothing shoulder, arm, and hand massage. You leave with brighter, smoother, more luminous skin and a clear, professional plan for maintaining it at home.

Whether you are preparing for a special occasion or simply investing in your skin's long-term health, this is the treatment our patients return for again and again.

What to Expect

The Experience, Step by Step

A thoughtful, unhurried sequence designed to treat your skin and let you truly unwind.

01

Consultation & Analysis

Your provider assesses your skin and builds a plan around your goals, concerns, and skin type.

02

Dermaplaning & Extractions

We gently remove dead skin and fine vellus hair, then clear congestion for a smooth, refined surface.

03

Customized Chemical Peel

A peel selected for your skin resurfaces and brightens, targeting texture, tone, and clarity.

04

LED Light Therapy

Targeted light wavelengths calm inflammation and stimulate the skin's natural renewal.

05

Custom Mask

A treatment mask is layered on to hydrate, soothe, and lock in the benefits of your facial.

06

Relaxing Massage

We close with a calming shoulder, arm, and hand massage — the perfect finishing touch.
